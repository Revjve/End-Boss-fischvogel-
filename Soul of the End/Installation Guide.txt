SOUL OF THE END
MythicMobs + ModelEngine boss.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Paper 26.2 or newer
  - MythicMobs 5.13
  - ModelEngine 4
  - Nexo (Optional, just to send resource pack)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
INSTALLING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Drop the contents of the "plugins" folder into your server's plugins
   folder.
2. Restart the server, then run:

       /meg reload
       /mm reload
       /nexo reload



--------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE MOBS - WHICH ONE DO I SPAWN?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

  fv_endsoul_block:  THE BLOCK. This is the one you spawn. It sits in your
                      arena looking like a normal block and cannot be hurt.
                      When a player LEFT or RIGHT clicks it, it wakes up
                      and turns into the boss.

  fv_endsoul:        THE BOSS (Soul of the End).

  fv_endsoul_bullet, fv_endsoul_burst, fv_endsoul_deathfx, and fv_endsoul_fx_base:
  Invisible effect helpers (projectile, explosion, death animation). Used by the skills - never spawn them.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
PLACING THE BOSS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Stand in the middle of your arena, on the floor, and spawn the block once:

    /mm mobs spawn fv_endsoul_block

For a perfect fit on the block grid, spawn it from the console at the
centre of a block (X and Z + 0.5, Y = the TOP of the floor block, e.g. 64
for a floor block at Y 63):

    mm mobs spawn fv_endsoul_block 1 world,100.5,64,200.5


The arena: an open, flat floor with at least 20 blocks of room around the
block. The boss never moves further than 18 blocks from the block (change
it in fv_endsoul_boss_settings)

To remove it:  /mm mobs kill fv_endsoul_block  (and /mm mobs kill fv_endsoul
if a fight is running).
