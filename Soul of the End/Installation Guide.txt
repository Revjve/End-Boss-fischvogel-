SOUL OF THE END - a soul trapped inside a block.
MythicMobs + ModelEngine + Nexo boss.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Paper / Purpur 1.21.1 or newer
  - MythicMobs 5.x
  - ModelEngine R4
  - Nexo (sends the resource pack with the models to your players)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
INSTALLING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Drop the contents of the "plugins" folder into your server's plugins
   folder, keeping the exact structure it comes in.
2. Restart the server, then run:

       /meg reload
       /mm reload
       /nexo reload all

Nexo builds the resource pack and merges ModelEngine's models into it
automatically - just make sure your players accept the pack. Every sound is
a vanilla Minecraft sound, so there are no sound files to install.

The pack only adds its own files:
  plugins/ModelEngine/blueprints/fv_endsoul.bbmodel  (the boss model)
  plugins/ModelEngine/blueprints/fv_endsoul_projectile.bbmodel
  plugins/MythicMobs/packs/fv_endsoul/  (mobs and skills)


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE MOBS - WHICH ONE DO I SPAWN?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

  fv_endsoul_block   THE BLOCK. This is the one you spawn. It sits in your
                      arena looking like a normal block and cannot be hurt.
                      When a player LEFT or RIGHT clicks it, it wakes up
                      and turns into the boss right where it stands.

  fv_endsoul         THE BOSS ("Soul of the End", with boss bar). Never spawn it
                      by hand - the block summons it and keeps track of it.

  fv_endsoul_bullet, fv_endsoul_burst, fv_endsoul_deathfx,
  fv_endsoul_fx_base
                      Invisible effect helpers (projectile, explosion, death
                      animation). Used by the skills - never spawn them.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
PLACING THE BOSS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Stand in the middle of your arena and spawn the block once:

    /mm mobs spawn fv_endsoul_block

The block drops onto the floor below the spot you spawn it at and centres
itself on that block (it appears half a second later), so it does not
matter if you were flying or standing half in the ground. Every height in the fight is measured from that floor.

That is all. The block is persistent: it survives restarts and remembers
whether a fight is running. When the boss is killed it is gone for good
(the block goes with it) - spawn a new block for another fight.

The arena: an open, flat floor with at least 20 blocks of room around the
block. The boss never moves further than 18 blocks from the block (change
it in fv_endsoul_boss_settings); it glides by teleporting, so pillars
inside that radius are passed straight through.

To remove it:  /mm mobs kill fv_endsoul_block  (and /mm mobs kill fv_endsoul
if a fight is running).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE FIGHT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE BLOCK
  It sits on the ground like a normal block and cannot be hurt. Up close you
  hear a heartbeat inside it, sometimes a whisper, and now and then it opens
  its eye and looks at you. Left OR right click it to wake it up.

WAKING UP (on_spawn, 7 seconds)
  The block turns into the boss in the same spot: light bursts out, it
  trembles, lifts off and keeps rising about two blocks into the air, the
  frame strains and bursts open - the screens of everyone within 24 blocks
  shake - and a shockwave shoves everybody back. Then it swoops down to eye
  level. It cannot be damaged during the whole animation.

THE LOOP
  It floats at eye level and keeps its eye on the closest player, but it
  does not sit still: between attacks it glides away from you, circles you
  sideways, drifts to a spot nearby or closes in when you keep your
  distance - starting slow, speeding up and easing out. If it strays too
  far from its block it turns and floats back. Then it picks an attack -
  never the same KIND (laser / down beam / shooting) twice in a row - and a
  random pattern of that kind.

  HIT REACTION: a hit while it is open (floating, moving, winding up an
  attack, shooting) knocks it back - the attack it was doing is cancelled,
  it plays its hurt animation, can do nothing for about 2.5 seconds, idles
  for a second and then backs away from you. It cannot be knocked back
  again until ~8 seconds after the hit (phase 2 / 3: ~9 / ~10), so you get
  one big interrupt at a time.

  Damage depends on what it is doing:
    floating, moving, winding up, shooting ... full damage (and the hit
                                                reaction above)
    laser beam or down beam live ............. half damage (its shell
                                                hardens, no hit reaction)
    landed and dazed after the down beam ..... x1.35 damage - the big opening
    screaming at a phase change .............. no damage (2 seconds)

  PHASE 2 at 66% health, PHASE 3 at 33%. When the current attack is done it
  screams and a shockwave throws everyone back. Every phase makes it faster:
  shorter idle, faster animations (110% / 120%), more and faster shots,
  quicker turns, shorter openings, and new patterns unlock. In phase 3 a
  shooting attack can chain straight into the next attack.

ATTACK 1 - LASER (attack1)
  It always pings ~0.4 s before the beam ignites. The beam reaches 10 blocks
  and hits hard (14).
  STRAIGHT   tracks you while charging, then the beam creeps after you at
             chest height - step sideways. Phase 2+: it pings again and
             FLICKS onto where you stand.
  JUMP ROPE  sinks low and sweeps 360 degrees - JUMP just as it reaches
             you: the top half of a jump clears it. The spin starts slow,
             speeds up and slows down again towards the end. A cyan ring
             on the floor shows the beam's reach. Phase 3: it reverses once
             halfway (high double click warns you).
  HUNTER     (phase 2+) follows you, turning faster every quarter of the
             beam. Circle it tightly or get out of its reach.
  CHAOS      (phase 2+) low sweep that randomly reverses (high double
             click) or changes speed (rising chime). Both are announced.
             Like the jump rope it starts slow and winds down at the end.

ATTACK 2 - DOWN BEAM (attack2)
  The cube rises, turns its eye to the floor and charges - a cyan ring
  marks the spot under it - then the beam fires straight down. Now it
  DASHES: it turns towards the closest player and draws a thin cyan line
  on the floor where it is going to charge. The line turns WHITE with a
  bell when the aim is locked - from then on it will not turn any more.
  A moment later it shoots along that line very fast (about 10 blocks in
  half a second) - jump or step to the SIDE. Three dashes per down beam;
  every phase locks on quicker. Standing under the beam burns like the
  laser (14). Its shell is hardened while the beam is on (half damage).
  Then it comes back down (a small shockwave when it lands) and hovers
  low, dazed: the big damage window.

ATTACK 3 - SHOOTING (shooting + the projectile model)
  Shots fly straight and level and burst where they end (direct hit 7,
  burst splash 3).
  AIMED      (phase 1-2) tracks you, PING + a thin cyan sight line, then a
             very fast shot.
  BURST      (phase 2+) a stream of shots aimed at you; every 3rd (phase 3:
             every 2nd) is aimed where you are GOING - change direction.
  RING       spins and sprays shots all around. Phase 2+: a second ring
             fires through the gaps of the first (phase 3: spinning back).
  FAN        (phase 2+) 5 shots wide, then 4 between them - step into gaps.
  SPIRAL     (phase 3) two streams sweep around it like clock hands, with
             gaps; it reverses halfway. Run around it close, or slip
             through a gap.

DEATH
  It lifts, the light rays spin out with a choir of every voice it held, it
  collapses, closes its eye, two heartbeats of silence - and the block
  shatters. The souls drift away. 1500 xp.

AFTERWARDS
  Once killed, the boss stays dead - there is no respawn. If everybody leaves (nobody within 40
  blocks for 15 seconds) the boss gives up and turns back into the block.
  After a restart or a chunk unload during a fight, the block simply
  re-forms.



--------------------------------------------------------------------------
TUNING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The balance values live in
plugins/MythicMobs/packs/fv_endsoul/skills/fv_endsoul_core.yml.

The most common changes:
  - boss health ............. Health: in mobs/fv_endsoul_mobs.yml (750 suits
                              1-3 players; add ~250 per extra player)
  - arena radius ............ fv_endsoul_boss_settings
  - damage numbers .......... the damage{amount=..} lines (listed in the
                              cheat sheet)
  - idle time, openings, how
    many shots, gaps ........ the PHASE TABLES (fv_endsoul_phase_1/2/3)
  - which patterns a phase
    may use ................. fv_endsoul_type_* in fv_endsoul_core.yml
  - projectile speeds ....... v= in the fv_endsoul_fire_* skills
                              (shooting file)
  - sounds .................. skills/fv_endsoul_fx.yml, one skill per moment
                              of the fight (fv_endsoul_sfx_...)

The animation speeds (speed=1.0/1.1/1.2 on the state{} lines in
fv_endsoul_anim_*) drive the tick numbers in fv_endsoul_phase_1/2/3: each
is the animation's own key moment divided by that phase's speed. If you
change a speed, redo that division.

The boss bar is BLUE on purpose: no vanilla boss uses that colour, so a
custom boss bar texture can replace it without touching the Ender Dragon,
Wither or raid bars.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
EDITING THE MODELS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

"Source Files" holds both models as Blockbench 5 projects. Edit them there,
then use "File > Export Legacy Project" and save over the matching file in
plugins/ModelEngine/blueprints, and run /meg reload. The blueprints use
Blockbench's legacy project format, which every ModelEngine build reads.

The skills rely on these animations by name: dormant, on_spawn, eyes,
idle, attack1, attack2, shooting, damage, death, blank (boss) and idle,
explosion, blank (projectile).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The block / boss is invisible
  Check that /meg reload printed no errors and that both .bbmodel files are
  in plugins/ModelEngine/blueprints. Players must accept the Nexo pack.

Projectiles or the explosion are invisible
  Set "Marker: false" in fv_endsoul_fx_base (mobs file) - a few ModelEngine
  builds will not render models on marker armour stands.

The block is stuck in the ground / bosses keep appearing
  Blocks spawned with an older version can sit inside the floor. Clear
  everything and spawn the block again (see PLACING THE BOSS):

      /mm mobs kill fv_endsoul_block
      /mm mobs kill fv_endsoul

  Only players wake the block now, and it never wakes while a boss is
  alive within 48 blocks.

Its attacks do not hurt me
  Creative and Spectator players are ignored by every attack - fight it in
  Survival or Adventure.

The boss does nothing after waking up
  Your difficulty is Peaceful (slimes are removed), or the chunk is not
  loaded. The fight needs Easy or higher.

The boss vanished / the block came back on its own
  Nobody was within 40 blocks for 15 seconds, the chunk unloaded, or the
  server restarted - in all three cases the fight resets on purpose.

Errors in the console mentioning fv_endsoul_...
  Please send me the lines; each skill is named after what it does.
