Hey, thank you very much for purchasing.

To use this pack install "MythicMobs", "ModelEngine" (R4) and "Nexo".
Minecraft 1.21.1 or newer (Paper / Purpur), MythicMobs 5.x.

Drop the contents of the "plugins" folder into your server's plugins folder,
keeping the exact structure it comes in. Then restart the server and run:

    /meg reload
    /mm reload
    /nexo reload all

Nexo builds the resource pack for you and merges ModelEngine's pack (the
models) into it automatically, so there is no separate resource pack to
install - just make sure your players accept the pack Nexo sends them.
Every sound in the fight is a vanilla Minecraft sound, so the pack adds no
sound files.

Nothing in the pack touches your existing configs: the MythicMobs files live
in their own pack (plugins/MythicMobs/packs/fv_endboss), the models in
ModelEngine's blueprints folder.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
PLACING THE BOSS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Stand in the middle of your arena and spawn THE BLOCK - once:

    /mm mobs spawn fv_endboss_dormant

For a perfect fit on the block grid, spawn it from the console with the
centre of a block (X and Z of the block + 0.5, Y = the top of the floor):

    mm mobs spawn fv_endboss_dormant 1 world,100.5,64,200.5

That is all. The block is persistent: it survives restarts and remembers
whether a fight is running or it is on cooldown. Never spawn "fv_endboss"
(the boss itself) by hand - the block summons it.

The arena: give it an open, flat floor with at least 20 blocks of room
around the block. The down beam never drifts further than 18 blocks from
the block (change it in fv_eb_boss_settings), but it glides by teleporting,
so pillars or walls inside that radius are passed straight through.

To remove it again:  /mm mobs kill fv_endboss_dormant  (and fv_endboss, if
a fight is running).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE FIGHT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

THE BLOCK
  It sits on the ground like a normal block and cannot be hurt. Up close you
  hear a heartbeat inside it, sometimes a whisper, and now and then it opens
  its eye and looks at you. Left OR right click it to wake it up.

WAKING UP (on_spawn, 7 seconds)
  The block turns into the boss in the same spot: light bursts out, it
  trembles, lifts off, speaks, the frame strains and bursts open and shoves
  everybody back. It cannot be damaged during the whole animation.

THE LOOP
  It floats in place and turns to face the closest player - this is when to
  hit it. Then it picks an attack - never the same KIND (laser / down beam /
  shooting) twice in a row - and a random pattern of that kind.

  When it takes damage depends on what it is doing:
    idle, winding up, shooting ......... full damage
    laser beam live .................... half damage (its shell hardens)
    up in the air for the down beam .... no damage (out of reach)
    landed and dazed after it .......... x1.35 damage - the big opening

  PHASE 2 at 66% health, PHASE 3 at 33%. When the current attack is done it
  screams and a shockwave throws everyone back. Every phase makes it faster:
  shorter idle, faster animations (110% / 120%), more and faster shots,
  quicker turns, shorter openings, and new patterns unlock. In phase 3 a
  shooting attack can chain straight into the next attack.

ATTACK 1 - LASER (attack1)
  It always pings ~0.4 s before the beam ignites. The beam reaches 10 blocks
  and hits hard (14).
  STRAIGHT   tracks you while charging, then the beam creeps after you at
             chest height - step sideways. Phase 2+: it pings again and
             FLICKS onto where you stand.
  JUMP ROPE  sinks low and sweeps 360 degrees at hip height - JUMP just as
             it reaches you: only the top of a jump clears it (easier the
             further away you stand). A red ring on the floor shows the
             beam's reach. Phase 3: it reverses once halfway (high double
             click warns you).
  HUNTER     (phase 2+) follows you, turning faster every quarter of the
             beam. Circle it tightly or get out of its reach.
  CHAOS      (phase 2+) hip-height sweep that randomly reverses (high double
             click) or changes speed (rising chime). Both are announced.

ATTACK 2 - DOWN BEAM (attack2)
  The cube rises 4 blocks, turns its eye to the floor and charges - a red
  ring marks the spot under it. Then the beam fires straight down into a
  glowing ring hovering just above the floor, and the boss slowly drifts
  after the closest player for 4 to 5 seconds. Standing in the ring burns
  like the laser (14). The beam reaches from the ring up to the cube, so
  jumping does not help - walk out of the ring and keep moving. It is slower than walking
  (phase 1 / 2 / 3: 2.2 / 2.8 / 3.4 blocks a second, players walk 4.3) and
  turns faster every phase. If it catches you it stops right on top of you;
  get behind it and it turns round on the spot before it follows again. It cannot be hit while it is up there. Then it
  comes back down (a small shockwave when it lands) and hovers low, dazed:
  the big damage window.

ATTACK 3 - SHOOTING (shooting + the projectile model)
  Shots fly straight and level and burst where they end (direct hit 7,
  burst splash 3).
  AIMED      (phase 1-2) tracks you, PING + a thin red sight line, then a
             very fast shot.
  BURST      (phase 2+) a stream of shots aimed at you; every 3rd (phase 3:
             every 2nd) is aimed where you are GOING - change direction.
  RING       spins and sprays shots all around. Phase 2+: a second ring
             fires through the gaps of the first (phase 3: spinning back).
  FAN        (phase 2+) 5 shots wide, then 4 between them - step into gaps.
  SPIRAL     (phase 3) two streams sweep around it like clock hands, with
             gaps; it reverses halfway. Run around it close, or slip
             through a gap.

DEATH
  It lifts, the light rays spin out with a choir of every voice it held, it
  collapses, closes its eye, two heartbeats of silence - and the block
  shatters. The souls drift away. 1500 xp.

AFTERWARDS
  The block re-forms after 5 minutes (fv_eb_block_settings; -1 = the boss can
  only be killed once). If everybody leaves (nobody within 40 blocks for 15
  seconds) the boss gives up and turns back into the block. After a restart
  or a chunk unload during a fight, the block simply re-forms.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
SOUND DESIGN - "A SOUL TRAPPED IN A BLOCK"
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Only vanilla Minecraft sounds, layered and pitched: its voice is ghasts,
vexes and the soul sand valley (wails, shrieks, whispers), its body is
breaking amethyst, glass, deepslate and chains, its heartbeat is the
warden's, its beams are beacons and conduits. Nothing to download, nothing
for players to miss if they decline the resource pack.

Which cue plays what is in skills/fv_endboss_fx.yml - one skill per moment
of the fight (fv_eb_sfx_...), so a sound is easy to swap.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT WAS CHANGED IN THE MODELS - functional only
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

The look of both models is untouched: geometry, textures, UVs, pivots and
the designed motion are exactly as delivered. These are the only changes:

  1. math.random() baked. Blockbench re-rolls math.random() every frame -
     that is what makes the shaker bones jitter. ModelEngine does not, so the
     jitter is pre-rolled into one keyframe per tick (20 per second, the
     most the game can show) within the very same ranges, using Blockbench's
     own interpolation rules. on_spawn, idle, damage, attack1, attack2,
     shooting and death; the laser flicker in attack1/attack2 too.
  2. idle: the eye cubes eye1/eye2/eye3 are hidden. All four eye cubes sit in
     the same spot; Blockbench draws eye4 on top, in game identical faces
     flicker. Hiding the three covered ones gives exactly the editor's look.
  3. A new animation "dormant": on_spawn's very first frame, frozen - the
     closed block, every bone pinned, the light rays and the small side
     lights tucked in. No new geometry; it is what the block shows before
     you click it, and waking up starts from exactly this frame.
  4. A new, empty animation "blank" (in both models). ModelEngine plays
     "default states" on its own - idle while a model stands still, walk
     while it moves, death when it dies. The skills point those at "blank"
     (and the block's at "dormant"), so only the fight's own animations
     ever move a bone.
  5. attack1 / attack2: the laser bone was set to "rotate in global space".
     Blockbench and ModelEngine handle that setting differently: in
     Blockbench attack2's beam points straight down at the floor, but
     ModelEngine ignores the cube flipping over, so the beam came out
     sideways. The same rotation is now written as plain keyframes (on the
     laser's parent bone) and the setting is off, so ModelEngine draws
     exactly what Blockbench shows: the beam straight down out of the eye,
     ending on the floor inside the glowing ring. A check in the build
     compares every corner of the beam, every tick, with Blockbench's pose
     (it matches to within one model pixel).
  6. "override" is ticked on every animation, so ModelEngine plays one
     animation at a time instead of adding idle on top of the attacks.
  7. Saved in Blockbench's legacy 4.10 format (the same as "File > Export
     Legacy Project" in Blockbench 5), which every ModelEngine build reads.
  8. Renamed to fv_endboss / fv_endboss_projectile.

Source Files contains your originals and the converted models in
Blockbench 5 format for editing. If you change an animation, save it, then
use "File > Export Legacy Project" over the blueprint in
plugins/ModelEngine/blueprints (or copy it there if your ModelEngine
version reads Blockbench 5 files).


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
TUNING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Everything worth balancing is listed in the "TUNING CHEAT SHEET" at the top
of plugins/MythicMobs/packs/fv_endboss/skills/fv_endboss_core.yml.

The most common changes:
  - boss health ............. Health: in mobs/fv_endboss_mobs.yml (750 suits
                              1-3 players; add ~250 per extra player)
  - respawn time ............ fv_eb_block_settings (seconds, -1 = once)
  - arena radius ............ fv_eb_boss_settings
  - damage numbers .......... the damage{amount=..} lines (listed in the
                              cheat sheet)
  - idle time, openings, how
    many shots, gaps ........ the PHASE TABLES (fv_eb_phase_1/2/3)
  - which patterns a phase
    may use ................. fv_eb_type_* in fv_endboss_core.yml
  - projectile speeds ....... v= in the fv_eb_fire_* skills (shooting file)

The animation speeds (speed=1.0/1.1/1.2 on the state{} lines in
fv_eb_anim_*) drive the tick numbers in the phase tables. If you change a
speed, redo the division written next to the table.

The boss bar uses the colour BLUE on purpose: no vanilla boss uses it, so a
custom boss bar texture can later replace the blue bar sprites without
touching the Ender Dragon, Wither or raid bars.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

Updating from an older version of this pack
  Replace BOTH .bbmodel files in plugins/ModelEngine/blueprints and the
  whole plugins/MythicMobs/packs/fv_endboss folder, run /meg reload,
  /mm reload and /nexo reload all, then kill the old block
  (/mm mobs kill fv_endboss_dormant) and place a new one. Older versions
  had custom sounds in plugins/Nexo/pack/assets/fv_endboss - that folder
  is not used any more and can be deleted.

The block / boss is invisible
  Check /meg reload printed no errors and that both .bbmodel files are in
  plugins/ModelEngine/blueprints. Players must accept the Nexo pack.

Projectiles or the explosion are invisible
  Set "Marker: false" in fv_endboss_fx_base (mobs file) - a few ModelEngine
  builds will not render models on marker armour stands.

The boss vanished / the block came back on its own
  Nobody was within 40 blocks for 15 seconds, the chunk unloaded, or the
  server restarted - in all three cases the fight resets on purpose.

The boss does nothing after waking up
  Your difficulty is Peaceful (slimes are removed), or the chunk is not
  loaded. The fight needs Easy or higher.

The down beam still points sideways
  The old blueprint is still loaded. Replace BOTH .bbmodel files, run
  /meg reload, then kill the old block and place a new one (see "Updating
  from an older version" above).

It glides through my arena's pillars
  The down beam moves by teleporting. Keep the floor within
  fv_eb_boss_settings' radius open and flat.

The boss sits too low / the low sweep never hits
  Every height in the fight is measured from the block's Y. Spawn the block
  standing on the floor, or from the console with Y = the TOP of the floor
  block (64 for a floor block at Y 63) - not inside the floor.

Errors in the console mentioning fv_eb_...
  Please send me the lines; each skill is named after what it does.
